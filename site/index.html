04 / Method

How we roast

01

We charge the drum at 195°C and let the beans fall to a turning point near 92°C. From there the rise is slow and even — no rushing the drying phase.

02

First crack arrives around 196°C at 8:40. We hold development to roughly 12% of total roast time, which keeps the acidity intact on the Ethiopians.

03

Drop at 9:50, cooled in under three minutes. Every batch is cupped on the Monday after roasting, and anything that misses the profile does not ship.

05 / Brew

Pour-over

Pour-over, 1:16

1:16 · 3:30

A single continuous pour after the bloom. Medium grind, 94°C, and a flat bed at the finish.

Immersion

French press, 1:15

1:15 · 4:00

Break the crust at four minutes, skim, then wait another five before pouring. Coarse grind, 96°C.

Espresso

Espresso, 1:2

1:2 · 0:28

18g in, 36g out, 28 seconds at 93°C. Grind finer for the washed lots, coarser for the naturals.
